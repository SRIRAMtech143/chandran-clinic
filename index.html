About the Doctor

Dr. R. Ravikumar

Dr. R. Ravikumar is a highly experienced general surgeon and family physician with over 23 years of dedicated medical practice since 2003. He has been serving patients at Chandran Clinic, Namakkal since 2012, bringing expert surgical and primary care under one roof.

Specializing in general surgery since 2020, Dr. Ravikumar has performed 500+ major surgeries and 3000+ minor surgeries with precision and care. As a complete family physician and surgeon, he provides comprehensive healthcare — from routine consultations to complex surgical procedures.

He believes in open communication, thorough pre-surgical consultation, and compassionate post-operative care for every patient he treats.

What We Treat

Surgical Services

Appendectomy

Safe and timely removal of an inflamed or ruptured appendix through open or laparoscopic techniques.

Hernia Repair

Treatment of inguinal, umbilical, and incisional hernias using both laparoscopic and open surgical approaches.

Gallbladder Surgery

Cholecystectomy for gallstones and gallbladder disease, performed with minimally invasive laparoscopic methods.

Colorectal Surgery

Surgical management of colorectal conditions including hemorrhoids, fissures, fistulas, and bowel resection.

Thyroid & Breast Surgery

Expert management of thyroid nodules, goiter, and breast lumps with careful surgical precision.
