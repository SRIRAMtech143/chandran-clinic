About the Doctor

Dr. R. Ravikumar

Dr. R. Ravikumar is a highly experienced general surgeon with over 21 years of dedicated practice. Specializing in both elective and emergency surgical procedures, he brings precision, empathy, and evidence-based care to every patient he treats.

Dr. Ravikumar has built a strong reputation for surgical excellence and compassionate patient relationships. He believes in open communication, thorough pre-surgical consultation, and comprehensive post-operative care.

Chandran Clinic, established in 2008, serves patients across the Namakkal region, offering a wide range of general surgical procedures in a safe and comfortable environment.

What We Treat

Surgical Services

Appendectomy

Safe and timely removal of an inflamed or ruptured appendix through open or laparoscopic techniques.

Hernia Repair

Treatment of inguinal, umbilical, and incisional hernias using both laparoscopic and open surgical approaches.

Gallbladder Surgery

Cholecystectomy for gallstones and gallbladder disease, performed with minimally invasive laparoscopic methods.

Colorectal Surgery

Surgical management of colorectal conditions including hemorrhoids, fissures, fistulas, and bowel resection.

Thyroid & Breast Surgery

Expert management of thyroid nodules, goiter, and breast lumps with careful surgical precision.

Trauma & Emergency Surgery

Immediate surgical intervention for abdominal trauma, bowel obstruction, perforations, and other emergencies.
